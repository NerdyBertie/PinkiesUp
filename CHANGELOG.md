# Pinkies Up!

## v1.0.0

### New
- A tea-sipping fish pops up with a silly "grats" every time you earn an achievement: "Pinkies up!", "You did the thing, and got the stuff!", "Boom shakalaka!", and 40-some more.
- Works in Retail and in WoW Forever (Legacy Challenges get a toast too).
- Feats of Strength get their own extra-fancy sayings.
- Click the toast to open the achievement, shift-click to link it in chat, right-click to dismiss, or drag it anywhere you like.
- Hover the toast to keep it on screen.
- Earn a bunch at once? They take turns, then a final "And X more!" so your screen doesn't flood.
- Optional grats line in your own chat window, with a link to the achievement.
- Settings under Options → AddOns → NerdyBertie → Pinkies Up!
