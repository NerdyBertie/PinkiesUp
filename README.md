# Pinkies Up!

**Every achievement deserves a little celebration.** Pinkies Up! sends a very proper, tea-sipping fish to congratulate you every time you earn an achievement, with a silly saying to match. Pinkies up, darling. You did the thing, and got the stuff!

Works in **Retail** and **WoW Forever**, where Legacy Challenges get a toast too.

## Features

- **A grats for every achievement.** A small toast pops up near the top of your screen with a random saying, the achievement's name and its points.
- **40-some sayings.** "Pinkies up!", "Boom shakalaka!", "Earl Grey-t job!", "Well, butter my crumpet!" and plenty more. The same one won't come up twice in a row.
- **Pretty gradient titles.** Every saying wears one of three soft gradients: Teatime, the fish's own colors, or Sunset Tea.
- **Feats of Strength get the fancy treatment.** A rainbow title and their own sayings. Zero points, infinite bragging rights!
- **Clickable.** Click the toast to open the achievement, or shift-click to link it in chat and show off.
- **Hover to keep it.** The toast waits while your mouse is over it.
- **Move it anywhere.** Drag the toast where you like. It remembers the spot.
- **No screen floods.** Earn a pile at once and they take turns, finishing with "And X more! Goodness!"
- **Optional chat line.** Also print the grats and an achievement link in your own chat window (only you see it).
- **Plays nice with Holiday Herald.** Holiday Herald has its own achievement toast. While that one is on, Pinkies Up! politely steps aside so you never get two. Turn that off in the settings if you'd rather have the fish do the honors.

## Commands

- `/pinkies`: open settings
- `/pinkies test`: show a test toast (handy for dragging it into place)
- `/pinkies test feat`: show a test Feat of Strength toast, rainbow and all
- `/pinkies reset`: move the toast back to its starting spot
- `/pinkies on` / `/pinkies off`: turn the toasts on or off
- Minimap button and addon compartment (the puzzle piece by the minimap): left-click for settings, right-click for a test toast

## Settings

Find them under **Options → AddOns → NerdyBertie → Pinkies Up!**: turn the toast on or off, show or hide the points, show or hide the minimap button, add a chat line, step aside for Holiday Herald, show a test toast, and reset the toast's position.

The AddOns and Options menus group my addons together, so you can find them all under the NerdyBertie heading.
